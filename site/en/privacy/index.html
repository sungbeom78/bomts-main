Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

The BOMTS public website currently does not provide account registration, payment, user tracking, or contact forms. Information sent directly by email is processed only to respond to the inquiry.

Information is used to respond to inquiries, protect the service, and investigate incidents. It is deleted when those purposes and any applicable legal retention obligations end. Personal information is not sold without a legal basis.
